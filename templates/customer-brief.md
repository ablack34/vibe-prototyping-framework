# Customer Brief

> **The customer's own voice.** This is the brief from the customer's perspective — their problem, in their words. Distinct from `engagement-brief.md`, which is the Studio 42 internal view.
>
> **How this document gets populated:**
>
> 1. **Ideal**: The customer sponsor fills this in themselves. Send the template, ask for half an hour of their time.
> 2. **Fallback**: If the customer can't write it, the VIBE Preparation agent extracts it from sales conversations, decks, RFPs, and meeting transcripts in `sources/`. Mark generated sections clearly and ask the customer to validate.
> 3. **Refresh**: Re-run `/vibe-customer-brief` any time new sources arrive. The brief should be living until Discover starts.
>
> **Tone**: First-person, plain language, no consulting-speak. If the customer wouldn't say it that way, rewrite it.
