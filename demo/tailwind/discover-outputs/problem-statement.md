## The statement

> **I am** a Tailwind Traders Returns Agent in the Bristol contact centre responsible for processing online returns in ReturnDesk.
>
> **I'm trying to** read the customer's free-text reason, choose the right reason code, spot possible wardrobing or serial-returner patterns, approve or deny the refund, and pick the right disposition — restock, refurbish, liquidate, or recycle — while keeping the customer human.
>
> **But** the reason-code dropdown is confusing, 41% of returns end up as "Other", I cannot see useful return-history patterns in the workflow, and I am guessing disposition from a desk for an item I cannot see.
>
> **Because** ReturnDesk is a 2012 system of record rather than an assistant: it shows a return row, not the product/condition/history reasoning an agent needs; fraud detection is manual and GDPR-sensitive; and the prior "smart" returns tool only printed labels, so the team does not trust black-box automation.
>
> **Which results in** average refunds taking 9 days instead of the under-3-day target, returns NPS sitting at -12 with 28% of returners saying they will not shop again, £2.3M/year in estimated wardrobing and abuse leakage, roughly 30% of items going to the wrong place, and Tailwind losing the analytics signal it needs because "Other" hides why products come back.
