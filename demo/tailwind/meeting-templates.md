## Scheduling tips (for the delivery team)

- **Always book the Disrupt Workshop first.** It's the hardest meeting to coordinate (most attendees, longest, in-person, customer-facing) and the rest of the schedule should anchor to it.
- **Block the Check-ins early** even if you don't yet know what you'll demo — calendar discipline matters more in Week 3 than meeting agenda discipline.
- **Travel days** for in-person workshops should be the day before, not the same morning. Studio 42 travels into Bristol on 2026-06-30.
- **Customer time zones**: the whole engagement is UK-based and all meetings are BST. There is no cross-region timezone spread to manage; keep the calendar simple and avoid inventing one.
- **September peak planning is the forcing function** — Elena needs board-ready evidence before the Black Friday/Christmas plan hardens.
