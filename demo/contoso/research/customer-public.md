## 7. Regulatory environment

- **EU F-Gas Regulation (revised, in force Q3 2026)** — refrigerant handling must be traceable to certified technicians. Penalties for non-compliance per work order. This directly motivates the "audit trail of why this tech was assigned" requirement in the customer brief
- **EU AI Act (in force Aug 2026)** — any AI system making employment-affecting decisions is high-risk. The Dispatcher AI concept must be framed as **assistive** (recommend-only, dispatcher accepts/rejects) to stay out of high-risk classification
- **GDPR** — technician location and skill data is personal data; processing requires DPA + retention policy
