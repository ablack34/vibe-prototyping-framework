## The statement

> **I am** a Senior Dispatcher at Contoso Field Services responsible for matching premium emergency HVAC calls to certified technicians across EMEA.
>
> **I'm trying to** dispatch the right technician — certified, available, with the right parts — within the 2-hour Platinum (or 4-hour Gold) SLA window, *and* warn the customer before the window slips when I can't.
>
> **But** I can't see SLA risk in time, and I can't see which technicians genuinely fit a job — only who is closest on a map.
>
> **Because** certifications, current-job ETAs, parts inventory, and F-Gas compliance live in four separate systems updated nightly or weekly, and our 2008-era dispatch tool only surfaces technician location.
>
> **Which results in** 30-40 SLA breaches per week (a quarter formally counted), costing ~€4M/year in contractual penalties and contributing to ~€3M/year in lost contract renewals (€6.3M over the last two years, per `customer-brief.md`) — and a steady drain on dispatcher trust and customer relationships.
