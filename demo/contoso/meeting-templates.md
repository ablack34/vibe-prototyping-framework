## Scheduling tips (for the delivery team)

- **Always book the Disrupt Workshop first.** It's the hardest meeting to coordinate (most attendees, longest, in-person, customer-facing) and the rest of the schedule should anchor to it.
- **Block the Check-ins early** even if you don't yet know what you'll demo — calendar discipline matters more in Week 3 than meeting agenda discipline.
- **Travel days** for in-person workshops should be the day before, not the same morning. Warsaw → Munich is a half-day with weather delays.
- **Customer time zones**: every Contoso attendee is CET. The Studio 42 squad spans GMT (London) and CET (Munich) — keep meeting times in CET to avoid the worst confusion.
- **Sandra is in NYC for an investor day during week 1** — keep her sessions in the afternoon CET so she can join from morning EDT.
