# What is VIBE Prototyping?

VIBE Prototyping is Studio 42's AI-first envisioning and prototyping service. We transform early-stage customer ideas into functional, test-ready prototypes — unlocking measurable business value at speed.

**You don't need to be technical, you just need a +1.**

## The Problem We Solve

| Challenge | What Happens |
|-----------|-------------|
| Vibe coding lacks quality | AI generates things fast that appear to work, but without human domain expertise they're unsustainable and insecure |
| Solving the wrong problem fast | Building faster doesn't matter if we're not solving problems that drive real business impact |
| AI without context is guesswork | Without trusted data, reliable code, and opinionated prompts, AI agents can't do their best work |

## What Makes VIBE Different

**Not another workshop.** We don't leave customers with post-it notes. We deliver working prototypes built in actual code, validated with real users.

**Not smoke and mirrors.** Our prototypes are grounded in feasibility, desirability, and viability. What the customer sees can become real.

**Not aimless coding.** We combine technical depth with business insight to create purposeful prototypes that accelerate presales.

## The Value Proposition

- **Tailored solutions** for unique customer challenges
- **Tangible deliverables** — functional prototypes, not slide decks
- **Access to Microsoft** design assets and engineering rigor
- **Reduced time-to-value** with actual code and real working prototypes
- **AI-powered** — every prototype demonstrates meaningful AI capabilities
