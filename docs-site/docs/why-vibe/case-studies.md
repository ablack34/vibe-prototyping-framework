# Case Studies

Real engagements demonstrating the VIBE approach in action.

## Siemens Healthineers — Diagnostic Imaging Partnership

**$19M deal · $140M expected consumption over 5 years**

### The Challenge

Radiology departments face pressure to maximize scanner utilization while maintaining diagnostic quality, safety, and staff well-being. Lead MTAs lack time to analyze performance data deeply amid daily operational demands.

### The VIBE Solution

**Teamplay AI** turns radiology data into proactive, actionable insights. Built on an agentic network integrated across the Teamplay ecosystem, it connects scanner, scheduling, and protocol data for continuous optimization.

### Studio 42's Impact

- Pre-sales and delivery
- Field research and product design
- Responsible AI assessment

> "S42 was instrumental to deliver experiences that cater for the high stakes environment we operate in." — Martin K., Product Manager

## Novartis — Synthetic Respondents

### The Challenge

Insights specialists need to evaluate creative concepts and behavioral hypotheses before investing in vendor-based research.

### The VIBE Solution

Simulated synthetic audiences (patients, healthcare professionals) that review early-stage marketing concepts. Personas parameterized by target audience, attitudinal profile, and behavioral traits — enabling "what if" testing before real research investment.

**Built with:** Copilot Studio, GitHub Copilot, VS Code, and GitHub Spark

## Coca-Cola Hellenic — Revenue Growth Management

### The Challenge

RGM teams operate in complex, deterministic systems with many audits, triggers, and interdependent decisions. High cognitive load, slow decision latency, and difficulty translating analyses into coherent leadership narratives.

### The VIBE Solution

AI-supported tools that help RGM teams explore what-if scenarios across pricing, promotions, channels, and volume — surfacing weak signals and understanding system-level interactions.

*This prototype was built and iterated live during an onsite workshop in Athens — successfully helping the customer articulate needs, priorities, and requirements in real time.*
