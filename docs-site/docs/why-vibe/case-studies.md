### Challenge

Radiology departments face relentless pressure to **maximize scanner utilization** while maintaining **diagnostic quality, safety, and staff well-being**. Although Siemens Healthineers' teamplay platform provides rich performance data, **Lead MTAs lack the time to analyze it deeply** amid daily operational demands. Performance reviews happen only periodically, leaving **untapped opportunities for continuous optimization** across the imaging fleet.

### Solution

**Teamplay AI** turns radiology data into **proactive, actionable insights**. It simplifies performance reporting while **detecting bottlenecks, underutilization, and recommending concrete improvements** or scanner specialization. Built on an **agentic network integrated across the teamplay ecosystem**, it connects scanner, scheduling, and protocol data for continuous optimization.

### Studio 42's Impact

Field Research · Product Design · Responsible AI · Pre-sales & Delivery

## Novartis — Synthetic Respondents

<div className="case-study-meta">
  <span className="case-study-tag">Pharma</span>
  <span className="case-study-tag">AI-Native</span>
</div>

### Challenge

Insights Specialists must evaluate creative concepts and behavioral hypotheses before investing in vendor-based research.

### Solution

Simulated **synthetic audiences** (patients, healthcare professionals) that review early-stage marketing concepts. Personas parameterized by target audience, attitudinal profile, and behavioral traits — enabling **"what if" testing** of concepts against different audience profiles.

**Built with:** Copilot Studio, GitHub Copilot, VS Code, and GitHub Spark

## Coca-Cola Hellenic — Revenue Growth Management

<div className="case-study-meta">
  <span className="case-study-tag">EMEA</span>
  <span className="case-study-tag">FMCG</span>
  <span className="case-study-tag">Live Workshop</span>
</div>

### Challenge

Revenue Growth Management (RGM) teams operate in highly complex, deterministic systems with many audits, triggers, and interdependent decisions. Decision-makers experience **high cognitive load, slow decision latency**, and difficulty translating fragmented analyses into coherent, defensible narratives for leadership.

### Solution

AI-supported tools that help RGM teams **explore what-if scenarios** across pricing, promotions, channels, and volume — **surfacing weak signals and potential triggers earlier** and understanding system-level interactions rather than isolated metrics.

> *This prototype was built and iterated live during an onsite workshop in Athens and was successful in helping the customer articulate needs, priorities, and requirements in real time.*
