## The Process at a Glance

| Phase | What Happens | Who | Duration |
|-------|-------------|-----|----------|
| **Discover** | Understand the problem through transcripts, docs, and research | Anyone | 1-3 days |
| **Disrupt** | Frame the value, prioritize use cases, lock requirements | Anyone | 1-2 days |
| **Ideate** | Brainstorm AI-powered concepts across form factors | Anyone | 1 day |
| **Build** | Engineer the prototype, deploy to Azure | Engineer | 5-10 days |
| **Deliver** | Generate roadmap, backlog, and handoff package | Anyone | 1-2 days |

**Most of the process doesn't require an engineer.** TPMs and designers drive Discover, Disrupt, and Ideate independently.
